Wash pass questions

What does the Bellagio Car Wash unlimited package offer?

Our membership grants unlimited access to our top-tier car wash services for a fixed monthly fee, ensuring your vehicle stays immaculate with every visit.

How do I sign up for an unlimited package?

Simply visit our location to enroll with no hassle.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancelling is easy and hassle-free. Just visit our website or speak with a staff member at our location to process your cancellation.

Do I pay extra for premium services?

Not at all. Your membership includes access to all our premium services, so you can always give your car the VIP treatment.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my package?

Upgrading or downgrading is a breeze. Simply log in to your account on our website or speak with our friendly staff at the car wash for assistance.

Can I share my package with family members?

Memberships are intended for individual use only, ensuring each member gets the full benefits and convenience.

Unlimited Wash Pass terms and conditions

Your credit card will be automatically charged on the same date of the month as your original sign-up date. Seven days advance notice prior to your Unlimited Wash Pass renewal date is necessary for cancellation or changes. Pass use is available during normal business hours. We reserve the right to close stores due to inclement weather, equipment issues or special events. Rates, terms and conditions are subject to change. One membership per vehicle is allowed. Fraud or tampering with the tag will result in termination of the pass.
